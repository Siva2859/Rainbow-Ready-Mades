## 2. Real Photography Guidelines
* **Authenticity:** All product photos must be taken directly at Rainbow Ready Mades under clear natural or shop lighting.
* **Aspect Ratio & Resolution:** 
  * Recommended resolution: $1000 \times 1200\text{px}$ (or $4:5$ portrait aspect ratio for clothing).
  * Format: WebP or compressed JPG ($\le 300\text{KB}$ per image for fast mobile loading).
* **Naming Convention:**
  * Primary product photo: `<product_id>_main.jpg` (e.g. `prod_001_main.jpg`)
  * Gallery angles: `<product_id>_detail.jpg`, `<product_id>_back.jpg`
  * Shop storefront: `shop_front.jpg`, `shop_interior.jpg`
