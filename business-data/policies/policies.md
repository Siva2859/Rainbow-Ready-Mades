# Official Store Policies: Rainbow Ready Mades

> **Source:** Rainbow Ready Mades Verified Business Records  
> **Date Verified:** 2026-09-19  
> **Applicable Store:** Rainbow Ready Mades, 12 Gandhi Road, Near Clock Tower  

---

## 1. Return & Refund Policy
* **Return & Refund Window:** Returns and exchanges are allowed within 10 days.
* **Refund Eligibility:** Refunds are allowed within the 10-day window.
* **Exchange Eligibility:** Exchanges are allowed within the 10-day window.
* **Damaged / Wrong Product:** Damaged or wrong-product cases are fully eligible for return, exchange, or refund.

---

## 2. Size & Color Exchange Policy
* **Exchange Window:** Exchanges are allowed within 10 days of purchase or delivery.
* **Exchange Options:** Customers may exchange items for a different size or color within the 10-day window.

---

## 3. Alterations & Tailoring Policy
* **Complimentary Services:** Complimentary length hem adjustments, sleeve adjustments, and minor waist tapering for garments purchased directly from Rainbow Ready Mades.
* **Turnaround Time:** Standard alterations take 24 to 48 hours. Express same-day alteration is available upon request at the store counter.

---

## 4. Shipping & Delivery Policy
* **Delivery Availability:** Delivery is available within a 10 km range of the store.
* **Coverage:** Covers addresses located within a 10 km distance from the physical shop.
* **Store Pickup:** Customers can choose "Store Pickup" at checkout to reserve garments and collect in person at the shop.

---

## 5. Payment Policy & Methods
* **Accepted Payment Modes:**
  * Cash
  * UPI
  * PhonePe
  * Google Pay
  * Card
  * Cash on Delivery (COD)
* **Currency:** All transactions are processed in Indian National Rupees (₹ / INR).
