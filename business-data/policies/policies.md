# Official Store Policies: Rainbow Ready Mades

> **Source:** Rainbow Ready Mades Store Management Physical Intake  
> **Date Verified:** 2026-09-18  
> **Applicable Store:** Rainbow Ready Mades, 12 Gandhi Road, Near Clock Tower  

---

## 1. Return & Refund Policy
* **Return Window:** 3 days from delivery date or in-store purchase date.
* **Refund Method:** Direct cash refunds and bank account refunds are **NOT** provided under any circumstances. We issue store credit or immediate product exchange for a different size/item.
* **Eligibility Requirements:**
  * Original brand tags, barcode tags, and price tags must be firmly attached and untampered.
  * Garment must be completely unworn, unwashed, unaltered, odorless, and in original condition.
  * Original cash receipt or online Order ID (e.g., `ord_8841`) must be presented.
* **Non-Returnable Items:**
  * Innerwear, undergarments, and thermal wear (hygiene policy).
  * Custom-tailored or altered garments.
  * Clearance or festival clearance rack items explicitly marked "Final Sale".

---

## 2. Size & Color Exchange Policy
* **Exchange Window:** 3 days from receipt of order or in-store purchase.
* **Exchange Procedure:**
  * Online: Request an exchange through the website order tracking page by selecting the order, item, and desired replacement size.
  * In-Store: Bring the garment with tags and original bill to Rainbow Ready Mades at 12 Gandhi Road.
* **Stock Contingency:** Exchanges are subject to rack and warehouse inventory availability. If the desired size or color is out of stock, the customer receives store credit valid for 6 months.
* **Differential Pricing:**
  * If exchanging for an item of higher price, the customer pays the remaining balance.
  * If exchanging for an item of lower price, the difference is issued as store credit.

---

## 3. Alterations & Tailoring Policy
* **Complimentary Services:** Free length hem adjustments, sleeve adjustments, and minor waist tapering for garments purchased directly from Rainbow Ready Mades.
* **Turnaround Time:** Standard alterations take 24 to 48 hours. Express same-day alteration is available for urgent events upon request at the store counter.
* **Note:** Once a garment is altered by our tailor or any external tailor, it becomes strictly non-returnable and non-exchangeable.

---

## 4. Shipping & Delivery Policy
* **Local Delivery Radius:** Rainbow Ready Mades delivers to addresses within a 15 km radius of our physical shop (including Gandhi Road, Clock Tower Market, Station Road, and surrounding localities).
* **Delivery Schedule:**
  * Orders placed before 3:00 PM are dispatched for same-day delivery.
  * Orders placed after 3:00 PM are delivered the next business day.
* **Shipping Charges:**
  * Orders of ₹999 or above receive **FREE Delivery**.
  * Orders below ₹999 incur a flat local delivery fee of ₹50.
* **Store Pickup:** Customers can choose "Store Pickup" at checkout to reserve the garment online and collect it in person at the shop at no extra charge.

---

## 5. Payment Policy & Methods
* **Accepted Payment Modes:**
  * Cash on Delivery (COD) for eligible local delivery zones.
  * UPI / QR Code payments (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) at the doorstep or in-store.
  * Cash and debit/credit cards accepted at the store counter.
* **Currency:** All prices and payments are strictly in Indian National Rupees (₹ / INR).
